About Me

My name is Jailen Davis. I was born and raised in Indianapolis, and I am currently a sophomore attending IU Indianapolis. My major is Media Arts & Science, and I am specializing in Video Production and Sound Design. I enjoy creating engaging videos through editing and utilizing camera equipment. I am passionate about telling stories, spreading messages, and entertaining through videos and sound, and I enjoy learning new techniques that can help improve my work. Alongside this, I am a drummer who enjoys playing music; it has always served as a way to clear my mind when I am stressed. My experience with music has strengthened my creativity and given me an engaging hobby alongside video work.
